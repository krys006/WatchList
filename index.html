DID YOU KNOW?

Fun Facts

🚀 INTERSTELLAR

Director Christopher Nolan worked with physicist Kip Thorne to make the black hole visual in the film scientifically accurate. The rendering took over 100 hours per frame. The film's depiction of time dilation near a black hole is consistent with Einstein's theory of relativity.

⚔️ RUROUNI KENSHIN

The live-action film series spans five films released between 2012 and 2021. Actor Takeru Satoh underwent rigorous sword-fighting training for months before filming. The final two films — The Final and The Beginning — were filmed simultaneously and released in the same year.

💡 House M.D. Trivia

Despite the famous running joke, the diagnosis actually IS lupus in one episode — Season 4, Episode 8. The writers finally gave fans what they'd been waiting for after seasons of misdirection.
