GENRE GLOSSARY

K-Drama
Korean television series known for high production value, emotional depth, and compelling romantic storylines. Usually 16–20 episodes per season.
C-Drama
Chinese-produced drama series, often set in historical imperial China with elaborate costumes, political intrigue, and sweeping romance.
Live-Action Adaptation
A film or series adapting an original anime or manga into real-world, live-filmed storytelling — like Rurouni Kenshin.
Procedural Drama
A genre where each episode features a self-contained problem alongside longer character arcs — as seen in House M.D.

A LITTLE MORE CONTEXT

These titles weren't just entertainment — they were experiences. Interstellar redefined what a science fiction film could feel like emotionally. Rurouni Kenshin's live-action trilogy proved that anime adaptations can be taken seriously. Scarlet Heart: Ryeo set an impossibly high bar for historical K-Drama. And House M.D. made medical diagnosis surprisingly addictive.

Pursuit of Jade is a hidden gem — if you haven't explored C-Drama yet, this is a great entry point.

DID YOU KNOW?

Fun Facts

🚀 INTERSTELLAR

Director Christopher Nolan worked with physicist Kip Thorne to make the black hole visual in the film scientifically accurate. The rendering took over 100 hours per frame. The film's depiction of time dilation near a black hole is consistent with Einstein's theory of relativity.

⚔️ RUROUNI KENSHIN

The live-action film series spans five films released between 2012 and 2021. Actor Takeru Satoh underwent rigorous sword-fighting training for months before filming. The final two films — The Final and The Beginning — were filmed simultaneously and released in the same year.

💡 House M.D. Trivia

Despite the famous running joke, the diagnosis actually IS lupus in one episode — Season 4, Episode 8. The writers finally gave fans what they'd been waiting for after seasons of misdirection.
